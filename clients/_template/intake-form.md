# Client Intake Form — [Business Name]

> Complete this before your onboarding call. There are no wrong answers — the more specific you are, the better we can represent you online. Write like you're talking to someone who doesn't know your business yet.

## Your Business & Story

**1. Tell us how [Business Name] got started. What's the origin story?**


**2. How do you describe what you do when someone asks at a job site, a trade show, or an event?**


**3. Walk us through what a typical client engagement looks like — from the first call to the work being done.**


---

## Your Customers

**4. Who is your ideal customer? Paint a picture of the person you love working with most.**


**5. Who is NOT your customer? (Types of jobs, clients, or situations you don't take on or that aren't a fit)**


**6. Where do most of your current customers come from? (Referrals, Google, word of mouth, repeat business?)**


---

## What Makes You Different

**7. What do you offer that your competitors don't — or don't do as well?**

**9. Is there anything about your business that surprises people once they learn more about it?**


---

## Goals

**10. What's the #1 thing you want to get out of this engagement?**


**11. What does success look like 6 months from now? What would make you say "this is working"?**


**12. Is there a specific type of customer, service, or geographic market you most want to grow into?**

## Brand & Voice
