## Audit Findings
- **Website Score:** 3/10 — Wix platform, zero H1 tags sitewide, meta descriptions missing on all service pages, outdated copyright (2021), broken template links
- **GBP Score:** 3/10 — Claimed but wrong category, only 3 reviews, website URL points to wrong page, no posts, no business description visible
- **SEO Score:** 2/10 — No H1s, schema is only a broken WebSite record named "afk-main", invisible for all target keywords, service pages have no meta descriptions
- **AI Search Score:** 1/10 — AI bots are permitted in robots.txt but content has zero structure for AI extraction; no FAQs, no answer blocks, no entity clarity
- **Social Score:** 4/10 — Present on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Twitter but activity unknown and branding is inconsistent across platforms

**Key Gaps:**
1. **No H1 tags on any page** — fundamental SEO failure. Every service page (Arrive & Drive, Race Prep, Trackside, LO-206) has zero H1 tags and no meta descriptions. Google has no clear signal about what each page is about.
2. **Wrong GBP category ("Racing car parts store")** — misclassifies the business entirely, groups them with indoor entertainment venues (K1 Speed, Buckeye Raceway), and confuses customers who think they sell parts. One 5-star review even describes it like a venue ("IT IS SO LIT IN HERE").
3. **Only 3 Google reviews in 19 years** — near-zero social proof. One is 1-star ("Answer your phone"). The business has a 3.7 average, which is damaging for a service business.
4. **Schema is named "afk-main"** — the only structured data Google has is a WebSite record with the internal Wix project name. No LocalBusiness, no Service schemas. Google doesn't know what this business is.
5. **GBP website URL points to /contact, not homepage** — anyone clicking through from GBP lands on a contact form with no context about who AFK is or what they offer.
6. **Footer contains a broken Wix template link** (http://www.twitter.com/wix) — a leftover from the Wix setup process. Visible to every visitor.
7. **Copyright says © 2013–2021** — signals a neglected site to both users and Google.
8. **Zero AI search presence** — no FAQ content, no structured answer blocks, no citable data, no entity clarity for AI systems.

**Quick Win:**
Fix the GBP category (from "Racing car parts store" to the most accurate available option — likely "Motorsports club" or "Go-kart racing team") and change the GBP website URL to the homepage. Takes 5 minutes and immediately corrects how Google categorizes and presents the business.
