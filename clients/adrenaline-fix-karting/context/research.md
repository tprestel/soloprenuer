# Research: Adrenaline Fix Karting

> Built by `deep-researcher` and `presence-auditor` from website analysis, GBP review, SEO data, and competitive research. All sections are our observations — not confirmed by the client.

## Business Context
Adrenaline Fix Karting (legal entity: Conlin SpeedSports, LLC) is a full-service kart racing team based in Columbus, OH — founded by Jim Conlin in 2007. They are NOT a go-kart entertainment venue and NOT a track operator. They build, prep, and crew racing karts for use at dedicated racing facilities, primarily New Castle Motorsports Park (NCMP) in Indiana. Their homepage includes an explicit disclaimer addressing this confusion. At ~$1.2M revenue with 4 employees and 19 years in operation, AFK is the largest active kart racing team in central Ohio — but nearly invisible online beyond people who already know them.

## Where They Operate
Shop/office: 516 Trade Rd, Columbus, OH 43204 (Mon–Fri 9AM–5PM, Sun 10AM–2PM). Primary on-track work: New Castle Motorsports Park (NCMP), New Castle, Indiana — the NCMP logo appears prominently in the site header. Also likely operate at other dedicated karting facilities in the Ohio/Indiana region.

**Geographic service reality:** NCMP is where customers physically go to race. This means AFK's true customer geography spans well beyond Columbus — any racer within ~2 hours of New Castle, IN is a qualified prospect. This includes Indianapolis (~45 min from NCMP), Dayton and Cincinnati (~1.5–2 hrs), Columbus (~2 hrs), and to a lesser extent Louisville KY and Detroit-area MI. **Keyword strategy must reflect this** — Indiana and Indianapolis terms are at least as important as Columbus terms. **Content must never imply AFK is a venue or that customers come to them to drive** — racing happens at NCMP and other dedicated tracks, not at the Columbus shop address.

## Who They Serve
Two inferred customer types:
1. **Hobby racers avoiding the maintenance burden** — people who love racing but don't want to own, transport, and maintain a kart. Arrive & Drive removes all that overhead.
2. **Competitive racers wanting professional support** — drivers already in the sport who need race prep, trackside crew, data analysis, and coaching to improve results.

Skill range runs from first-time racers ("first step in the pursuit of a career in motorsports") through serious competitors. The site welcomes both without talking down to either.

## What Makes Them Different
- **19-year tenure** — one of the most established karting operations in central Ohio
- **Full-service operation** — coaching, data acquisition, prep, trackside crew all under one roof; not just rentals
- **LO206 specialization** — dedicated engine class page signals class-specific expertise
- **Data-driven approach** — post-session data review mentioned as standard practice
- **Industry relationships** — NCMP, MAGiK Kart, GreyHound Racing Seats, PaRacing visible as sponsors/partners in the header

## Common Misconceptions
The site addresses this directly with a homepage disclaimer (verbatim):
> "...we do not have a kart track of our own as some search engines suggest. Also, we only specialize in building, maintaining and supporting full racing karts for use at dedicated kart racing facilities. Due to liability issues we are unable to service 'yard karts' or 'fun karts'."

Key misconceptions to counter in all content:
1. They are NOT a go-kart entertainment venue (not like K1 Speed or Buckeye Raceway)
2. They do NOT own or operate a track
3. They do NOT service yard karts or fun karts
4. Their Columbus address is a shop — racing happens at NCMP (Indiana) and other dedicated facilities

## Brand Voice (Inferred)
Tone: Direct, passionate, knowledgeable — not corporate, not overly casual. Writes as if the reader has some motorsport context; doesn't dumb it down.

Phrases used naturally: "get you out there," "your fix," "green flag," "pit crew," "data acquisition," "arrive and drive," "high-speed, high-intensity"

Phrases/language to avoid: fun-kart language ("go-kart fun," "family activity," "no experience needed"), venue language ("come visit us," "open track days"), indoor entertainment framing

## Competitive Landscape
- **Trinity Karting Group** — `trinitykartinggroup.com` — Full-service kart racing team based in Batavia, OH (Cincinnati area). Operates at Motorsports Country Club of Cincinnati. Runs SKUSA, WKA, and KRA programs. Year-round driver development. Strong digital presence. Confirmed competitor.
- **A-Maxx Racing** — `amaxxracing.com` — Based in Whiteland, Indiana (Indianapolis suburb). Competes at Whiteland Raceway Park (IKC series). Offers tent space, transport, data analysis, driver coaching, and tuning — almost identical service model to AFK. Direct Indiana-market competitor.
- **NEWGEN Motorsport** — `newgenmotorsport.com` — Based in Whiteland, IN. Offers arrive & drive, kart prep, rebuilds, coaching, and data. Competes at Whiteland Raceway Park AND New Castle Motorsports Park — the same track AFK operates at. Closest direct overlap with AFK's actual racing geography.
- **Previously listed as "Racing Ready"** — Removed. No evidence this is a real company. Likely hallucinated by competitive brief tool.
- **Previously listed as "EFM Racing"** — Removed. No evidence this is a real company. Likely hallucinated by competitive brief tool.
- **ProCup Karting** — `procupkarting.com` — East Coast arrive-and-drive event organizer, not a team operation. Not a direct competitor.
- **What confirmed competitors do better:** All three have clearer digital positioning and stronger GBP presence than AFK. NEWGEN and A-Maxx both operate at Indiana tracks and capture Indianapolis-area search traffic that AFK is entirely absent from.
- **Structural disadvantage:** AFK's wrong GBP category ("Racing car parts store") groups them with indoor entertainment venues rather than racing teams — confuses search intent entirely
- **Gap AFK can own:** The Columbus-to-NCMP corridor is unclaimed. Trinity is Cincinnati-focused. A-Maxx and NEWGEN are both Indianapolis/Whiteland-focused. No competitor is positioned for the Ohio racer who drives to Indiana to compete — that's AFK's market.

## SEO Opportunity
- **Authority:** Near-zero organic authority — brand name only rankings
- **Current ranking keywords:** "Columbus go karts" positions 18–53 (scattered, no real traffic); brand name only
- **Key gaps:** Not ranking for any high-intent competitive karting terms despite being the dominant operator in the region — and critically, the keyword strategy was previously anchored only to Columbus OH. Since racing happens at NCMP in Indiana, Indianapolis and Indiana-wide terms represent an equally important (and less competitive) opportunity.

## Opportunity Summary
AFK is a 19-year, $1.2M/year business and the largest active kart racing team in central Ohio — but Google doesn't know what they are. Their GBP has the wrong category (parts store, not racing team), they have zero H1 tags sitewide, their only schema is named "afk-main" (an internal Wix label), and they have 3 reviews in 19 years. 720 people/month search "go kart racing Columbus Ohio" and AFK doesn't appear once. Pitch angle: "People are already looking for exactly what you do — but right now Google doesn't even know what your business is."
