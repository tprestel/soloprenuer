About AFK

Built on Real Racing Experience

Nineteen years in this sport means we've seen every engine class come and go. We've worked with drivers at every level — from someone who's never been on a race track to drivers competing seriously every season. We do everything in-house. No farmed-out engine work. No third-party kart builds.

We build the kart, we prep it, we haul it to the track, we crew it during the race, and we debrief the driver afterward with data. You show up, you drive, you go home.

"The same people who built your kart are standing in your pit."

— Jim Conlin, Founder, Conlin SpeedSports, LLC
