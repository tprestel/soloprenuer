What We're Fixing

Five Problems Between You and the Drivers Already Looking for You

1

Google Thinks You Sell Car Parts

Business impact: Every person who finds your Google listing sees a business categorized as a "Racing car parts store." They're expecting an auto parts shop, not a professional racing team. The customers who ARE looking for a kart racing team never see you.

Finding: GBP category set to "Racing car parts store." Website URL points to /contact instead of homepage. No business description. 3 reviews in 19 years — one is 1-star ("Answer your phone"). Rating: 3.7.
Fix: Correct category, homepage link, full description, review strategy, GBP posting schedule.
2

Your Website Gives Google Nothing to Work With

Business impact: Without H1 tags or meta descriptions, Google can't determine what any of your pages are about. Your service pages — Arrive & Drive, Race Prep, Trackside Support — are invisible to search.

Finding: Zero H1 tags sitewide. Meta descriptions missing on all service pages. Copyright says © 2013–2021. Footer links to twitter.com/wix — a Wix template leftover.
Fix: Full on-page SEO — H1s, meta descriptions, title tags, keyword mapping on every page.
3

Google Doesn't Know What Kind of Business This Is

Business impact: Schema markup tells search engines what your business is in their language. Right now Google has nothing except an internal Wix project name.

Finding: Only schema is a WebSite record named afk-main. No LocalBusiness schema. No Service schemas. No structured data of any kind.
Fix: LocalBusiness + Service schemas, keyword-mapped page structure, entity clarity content.
4

AI Search Can't Find or Cite You

Business impact: When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI "where can I do arrive and drive karting near Indianapolis," your business doesn't exist in their answers.

Finding: AI bots are permitted in robots.txt, but the site has zero FAQ blocks, zero answer-formatted content, and no entity clarity. AI Score: 1/10.
Fix: FAQ blocks, structured answer content, and entity clarity — built into the new site.
5

The Wix Platform Is a Ceiling

Business impact: Wix generates JavaScript-heavy pages that search engines struggle to crawl. The template has broken links, outdated design, and no CRM. Patching approaches the cost of replacing it — without removing the constraint.

Finding: Wix platform with crawl limitations. Broken template link in footer. No lead tracking — every inquiry goes to email with no follow-up system.
Fix: Rebuild on HubSpot — CRM, lead tracking, and a site built for search from day one.
