Three Pairings to Consider

Finding the Right
Type for AFK

Each option below shows the font in context — real AFK copy, real brand colors, styled the way it would actually appear on the site. The goal: a typeface that reads as serious motorsport without shutting out newcomers. All fonts are Google Fonts, free to use with no licensing overhead.

Option One
Chakra Petch
+ DM Sans

Angular, technical, precise. Feels like a race team's data system — engineering authority without the cold industrial edge. The slight geometric slant reads "precision" rather than "tech startup." Best fit for AFK's data-driven, expert-forward positioning.

Option Two
Racing Sans One
+ Outfit
★  Recommended

Designed for motorsport. Racing Sans One is unapologetically a race font — condensed, punchy, no ambiguity. Outfit keeps the body warm and readable without undercutting the energy. The best balance of accessibility and authority for where AFK is positioned.

Option Three
Big Shoulders Display
+ Outfit

Aggressive and editorial. Big Shoulders is Chicago-flavored — wider than your typical race font, which makes it stand out. Strong enough to anchor a homepage, distinctive enough to be remembered. Pushes AFK toward a more graphic, publication-style identity that none of its competitors are using.
