## Founding Story

Jeff Hill has been in the trailer industry since 1996. In 2012 he began racing karts in Indiana, which deepened his firsthand understanding of the trailer world — how people work, live, and travel in them. In 2016 he turned his passion and expertise into a business, selling his first trailer and launching Jeff Hill Trailer Sales. His racing background is not incidental — it is the core brand identity and the basis for his custom-design expertise.

## Target Audience

The site copy and product mix suggest a broad Midwestern buyer: anyone who needs a trailer — from karting/motorsport enthusiasts (Jeff's own community) to contractors, small business owners, food vendors, campers, and hobbyists hauling ATVs or motorcycles. The "family" language, rural Indiana location, first-person casual voice, and Facebook-forward social strategy all signal a working-class, practical, community-oriented buyer who values trust and personal relationships over slick dealer experiences.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

> B2C — direct consumer sales.

- **Age Range / Life Stage:** Adults 25–60; working-age, likely homeowners or small business operators
- **Income / Lifestyle Indicators:** Working-class to middle-class; price-conscious but quality-aware; active outdoor, motorsport, or contractor lifestyle; Facebook-primary social media users
- **Psychographic Profile:** Values straight talk, personal relationships, and no-BS buying experiences; skeptical of corporate dealerships; takes pride in their work/hobby equipment; likely to travel to buy if they trust the seller
- **Trigger Event:** Buying a first enclosed trailer for karting/racing, starting a food truck business, launching a landscaping company, upgrading equipment hauling capacity, or replacing a trailer
- **Purchase Behavior:** One-time large purchase; financing commonly used; trade-ins common; willing to call/text directly vs. filling out web forms
- **Geographic Reach:** National (confirmed 48 states) despite rural Indiana base — suggests word-of-mouth and Facebook as primary reach channels
- **Disqualifiers:** Buyers needing gooseneck/livestock/horse trailers (limited current inventory); buyers who only shop large-volume dealers
- **Confidence Level:**

  - Geographic reach 48 states: High (explicitly stated in news profile)
  - Racing/motorsport as core segment: High (founder story, site copy)
  - Small business/contractor segment: Medium (concession + dump trailers in inventory)
  - Financing users: High (mentioned on virtually every product listing)

## Brand Positioning

- **Category:** Independent trailer dealership (Midwest, rural Indiana)
- **Primary Differentiator:** Owner with deep racing and industry background offers personalized, no-BS custom design help — no hidden fees, no pushy sales tactics
- **Tone of Positioning:** Approachable, community-driven, expertise-led, anti-corporate
- **Key Competitors:**
  - **Indiana Trailer Sales** (Decatur, IN) — large multi-brand dealer, inquiry-driven, 25+ brands
  - **TC Trailers** (Michigantown, IN) — "one of central Indiana's largest," 400+ inventory, 30+ years family-owned, includes dedicated race trailers category
  - **Trailer Sales of Indiana** (Shelbyville, IN) — volume dealer, 600+ in stock, claims biggest Lamar/Load Trail dealer in Indiana
  - **Spencer Trailers** (Spencer, IN) — 50+ year family business, dealer-of-the-year awards, 450+ in stock, full service
  - **Weaver's Trailer Sales & Service** (Middlebury, IN) — custom design tool, racing and horse trailer niche, fabrication/welding
- **Positioning vs. Competitors:** Jeff Hill is the smallest operation among these competitors but has the clearest owner-identity story and the most specific niche authority (racing/motorsport). Competitors win on scale and brand breadth; Jeff Hill wins on personal trust and custom expertise.
