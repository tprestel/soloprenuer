## Conversion Architecture

### Primary Conversion Action
**Phone call to Jeff Hill (765-744-6214).** The owner's personal number is embedded in the logo and repeated throughout the site. The site is structured to push visitors toward calling or messaging, not purchasing directly online — despite having BUY NOW buttons.

### Full Conversion Map

| Location | Action | Offer / Ask |
|----------|--------|-------------|

| Homepage product grid | "View All Products" link | Inventory browse |

| Product listing pages | "BUY NOW" + "ADD TO CART" | Online purchase (GoDaddy e-commerce) |
| Contact page | Contact form (Name, Email, Message) | Quote request / inquiry |
| Contact page — APPLICATION | "Files coming soon" | Financing application — not yet live |
| Footer (all pages) | Email subscribe field | Email list signup |
| Contact page form | Checkbox: "Sign up for our email list" | Email list signup at point of inquiry |

### Lead Magnets & Gated Content
None. No downloadable guides, buyer's checklists, financing calculators, or gated assets of any kind.

### Conversion Gaps
1. **No financing application live** — The APPLICATION section on the contact page says "Files coming soon." Financing is referenced on nearly every product listing as a selling point, but there's no way to start the process online. High-friction for a common buyer need.
2. **Primary hero CTA sends traffic to Facebook** — The homepage hero sends visitors away from the site to Facebook. No on-site conversion captures this initial intent.
3. **No live chat or text messaging widget** — Given that Jeff's personal cell is the primary conversion path, a click-to-text or SMS widget would dramatically lower friction.
4. **Blog has no CTAs** — Blog does not exist, so there are no mid-funnel content readers being captured.
5. **Email subscribe has no stated value proposition** — The footer just says "Sign up for our email list for updates, promotions, and more." No reason to sign up, no incentive.
6. **No testimonials on-site** — 5.0/20 Google reviews exist but are not displayed anywhere on the website. Major trust signal going unused.

### Competitive Gaps & Opportunities

1. **SEO is wide open.** None of the competitors appear to be running aggressive content SEO programs. Jeff Hill has a compelling story and niche expertise (racing) that none of them own — a content strategy built around racing/karting trailers, custom trailer design guides, and Indiana-focused local SEO could establish category authority no competitor has claimed.
2. **No competitor leads with a founder story the way Jeff Hill can.** TC Trailers and Spencer Trailers note family ownership but not personal passion stories. Jeff's racing origin is authentic and differentiating — a brand story asset no competitor can copy.
3. **Facebook Live is underused by competitors.** Jeff is already doing Facebook Live — this is a real-time trust-builder that volume dealers can't replicate with the same intimacy. Systematizing and promoting it could drive significant engagement.
4. **Review volume gap.** Jeff has 20 Google reviews at 5.0 stars — excellent quality but low quantity vs. established dealers. A review generation campaign could rapidly build social proof.

## Growth Marketing Observations

1. **Facebook is the core acquisition channel.** The hero CTA links to Facebook, not a landing page. The 3,483 Facebook followers represent a meaningful audience that was built before the website had any SEO presence. Facebook is where this business lives.
2. **The website functions as a product catalog, not a marketing engine.** With 4 indexed pages, no blog, and no SEO content, the website cannot generate organic traffic at scale. All current traffic is either direct (people who already know Jeff) or social-referral (Facebook/Instagram).
3. **Financing is a major selling point with zero infrastructure.** "Financing available" appears on nearly every product listing, but there's no application, no partner mentioned, no process described. Completing this funnel is likely a high-ROI quick win.
4. **The review profile is a hidden asset.** 5.0 stars / 20 reviews is an excellent signal, but it's invisible on the website. Featuring Google reviews prominently on the homepage and product pages would convert more visitors.
5. **Paid ads are being used but not optimized.** The April 2025 ad spent under $100 over 9 days. The current March 2026 ad is similarly small-budget. There's no evidence of A/B testing, landing page optimization, or audience targeting strategy. Paid ads appear to be experimental rather than a reliable acquisition channel.
