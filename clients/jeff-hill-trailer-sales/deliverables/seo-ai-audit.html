Technical SEO Findings

Only 4 of 71+ sitemap URLs are indexed by Google Critical
The sitemap contains 10 website pages + 61 e-commerce URLs (product pages, category pages). Google has indexed only 4 of them. This means every product listing, every brand category page, and almost every page on the site is invisible to search engines. The most likely cause: GoDaddy's e-commerce pages are rendered by JavaScript, and Google is not fully crawling and rendering the store pages. This is the single most damaging technical issue on the site.
Evidence site:jeffhilltrailersales.com returns 4 results. Sitemap contains 71+ URLs. SEMrush shows only branded keyword rankings.
Recommended Fix
Migrate to a platform with server-side-rendered product pages (Shopify, WordPress/WooCommerce, or a custom site). On GoDaddy, submit sitemap to Google Search Console, request indexing of key pages manually, and monitor the Coverage report. This is a platform limitation that cannot be fully solved within GoDaddy Website Builder.
No canonical tags on any page Critical
Not a single page on the site has a canonical tag. Without canonicals, Google has no guidance on which version of a URL is authoritative. This matters especially because the two sitemaps use different homepage URL formats: https://jeffhilltrailersales.com/ (with trailing slash) and https://jeffhilltrailersales.com (without) — these are treated as separate URLs and create duplicate content signals.
Recommended Fix
Add <link rel="canonical" href="[page-url]"> to every page's <head>. Standardize all URLs to consistently use the trailing-slash format. On GoDaddy, check if canonical tag injection is available in site settings or page SEO options.
No schema markup / structured data anywhere on the site Critical
Zero JSON-LD schema was found on any page. For a local business with a physical lot, this means: no LocalBusiness rich results, no map pack enhancements, no Product rich results on product listings, no Review stars in search results (despite having 20 Google reviews). Schema markup also provides a 30–40% boost to AI citation visibility.
Missing LocalBusiness, Product, Organization, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage schemas
Recommended Fix
At minimum, add LocalBusiness schema to the homepage with: name, address, phone, URL, geo coordinates, openingHours, and priceRange. Add Product schema to each product listing with: name, description, price, availability, and brand. Use Google's Rich Results Test to validate after implementation.
Google Business Profile — status unconfirmed and possibly unclaimed Critical
For a local trailer dealership, Google Business Profile is the single highest-ROI SEO action available. It controls whether the business appears in Google Maps, the local pack (the 3-box result shown above organic results), and "near me" searches. The GBP status could not be confirmed as fully claimed and optimized. A Google Maps pin was found but hours, photos, posts, products, and Q&A sections are unknown.
Recommended Fix
Claim and verify the Google Business Profile at business.google.com. Complete: business category ("Trailer Dealer"), hours, phone, website, description (include keywords like "enclosed trailers," "cargo trailers," "dump trailers"), add 10–20 photos of inventory and the lot, add products, enable messaging, and post weekly.
Robots.txt has no Sitemap directive High
The robots.txt file contains only User-agent: * — a single line. The Sitemap URL is not referenced, which means Google's crawler has to discover the sitemap independently or via Search Console submission. This is a missed crawl-guidance signal.
Recommended Fix
Add Sitemap: https://jeffhilltrailersales.com/sitemap.xml to robots.txt.
Account management URLs included in sitemap High
The sitemap includes /m/login, /m/reset, /m/create, and /m/create-account — customer account pages with zero SEO value. Worse, SEMrush shows Google is actually indexing /m/login and /m/create-account and ranking them for the branded keyword "jeff hill trailer sales" at positions 21 and 27. This wastes crawl budget and dilutes the brand's SERP presence.
Recommended Fix
Add noindex meta tags to all /m/ pages and exclude them from the sitemap.
Sitemap lastmod dates are stale (January 2025) High
The website sitemap shows a lastmod of 2025-01-25 for all 10 website pages. This signals to search engines that the site content has not been updated in over a year. Dynamic sitemaps should reflect actual content update dates.
Recommended Fix
Ensure the sitemap is dynamically generated and reflects accurate lastmod dates. On GoDaddy, this may update automatically when pages are saved — re-save all key pages to trigger an update.
OG description tag contains literal asterisks "****" Medium
The homepage og:description meta tag contains only asterisks (*******), which is a redacted Meta ad copy artifact. When this page is shared on social media, the preview description will show asterisks instead of a meaningful business description.
Recommended Fix
Set the OG description to a real value: "Jeff Hill Trailer Sales — enclosed cargo trailers, utility trailers, dump trailers, and concession trailers in Modoc, Indiana. Owner-operated since 2016. No hidden fees."
GoDaddy Website Builder — fundamental SEO platform limitations Medium
The site is built on GoDaddy Website Builder (Starfield Technologies). This platform has known limitations for SEO: JavaScript-rendered pages that Google struggles to crawl, limited schema control, no canonical tag injection, and constrained URL structure customization. Most critical SEO improvements will require either working around these limits or migrating to a more SEO-capable platform.
Recommended Fix
For a business where the website is the primary sales channel, consider migrating to Shopify (best for e-commerce trailer inventory) or WordPress + WooCommerce. Shopify natively handles canonical tags, sitemap generation, and has strong schema support. This is a 6–12 month strategic recommendation, not an immediate fix.

On-Page SEO Findings

Homepage title tag has no descriptive keywords or location Critical
The current homepage title is simply Jeff Hill Trailer Sales — just the brand name with no keywords, no product description, and no location. Title tags are the most weighted on-page SEO signal. Anyone who doesn't already know the business name would find nothing relevant in this title.
Current Jeff Hill Trailer Sales
Suggested Enclosed Trailers & Cargo Trailers for Sale | Jeff Hill Trailer Sales — Modoc, IN
Recommended Fix
Rewrite the homepage title to lead with the primary product category and include the location. Keep under 60 characters for full SERP display. Update in GoDaddy page settings under SEO title.
No meta descriptions on any page Critical
Zero pages have a meta description. When Google generates snippets for pages without descriptions, it picks arbitrary text from the page — often poorly optimized and unappealing. Meta descriptions don't directly affect rankings, but they directly affect click-through rate from search results.
Pages Homepage, Shop, Contact, Gallery, all product pages — none have meta descriptions
Recommended Fix
Write a unique meta description for every page (150–160 characters). Homepage example: "Browse enclosed cargo trailers, dump trailers, concession trailers & more at Jeff Hill Trailer Sales in Modoc, Indiana. Financing available. No hidden fees. Call 765-744-6214."
Shop page: no H1, keyword-free title, no meta description High
The main inventory page (/shop-in-stock-trailers) has: title tag SHOP IN STOCK TRAILERS (all-caps, zero location or product keywords), no H1 whatsoever, and no meta description. This is the page where buyers land after clicking — it has the best chance of ranking for commercial keywords if optimized.
Current title SHOP IN STOCK TRAILERS
Suggested title Trailers for Sale in Indiana | Enclosed, Cargo & Utility | Jeff Hill Trailer Sales
Recommended Fix
Add an H1 ("Trailers for Sale in Indiana"), write a keyword-rich meta description, and optimize the title tag. Add a 2–3 sentence intro paragraph above the product grid with location and product keywords.
H1 heading hierarchy issues on contact and shop pages High
The Contact page H1 is CONTACT (a single generic word), and the Shop page has no H1 at all. The homepage H1 is Join the Jeff Hill Trailer Sales family today. — brand-oriented rather than keyword-oriented. Each page should have one H1 that contains the primary target keyword for that page.
Recommended Fix
Homepage H1: Keep existing (brand identity works here, keywords go in title). Shop H1: "Trailers for Sale in Indiana." Contact H1: "Get a Quote on Your Next Trailer."
5.0★ Google reviews not displayed anywhere on the website High
Jeff Hill has 20 Google reviews at a perfect 5.0-star rating — an exceptionally strong trust signal. None of this social proof appears on the website. Visitors who land on the site from any channel (ads, direct, referral) have no on-site evidence of customer satisfaction. This is a conversion killer and an E-E-A-T signal Google values highly.
Recommended Fix
Add a reviews/testimonials section to the homepage with embedded quotes from real Google reviews. Add a Google Reviews widget or badge. Add Review schema markup to enable star ratings in search results.
No content targeting category-level or informational searches Medium
The entire site is either a product catalog or a contact page. There are no location pages, no brand/type category pages with SEO copy, and no blog. This means the site can only rank for transactional queries with exact product matches — it cannot capture buyers at the research stage or establish topical authority.
Recommended Fix
Create SEO-optimized category landing pages for: "Enclosed Cargo Trailers for Sale in Indiana," "Dump Trailers Indiana," "Concession Trailers for Sale," and "Race/Kart Trailers Indiana." Each page needs 300–500 words of optimized copy, H1, meta description, and internal links to matching inventory.
No location-specific landing pages Medium
The business serves Modoc, Indiana and has sold trailers in 48 states. There are no location pages targeting nearby cities: Muncie (30 min), Richmond, Anderson, or Indianapolis. Local SEO capture requires location-specific content beyond just having the address on a contact page.
Recommended Fix
Create a primary "Trailers for Sale in Indiana" page. Add location-mention content to key pages referencing Modoc, Randolph County, east-central Indiana, and major nearby cities.

AI Search Visibility Audit

🤖
Zero AI citations found across all platforms and query types
Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI platforms did not cite Jeff Hill Trailer Sales for any tested query. The site lacks the structural, authority, and content signals that AI systems use to select sources. AI search is now present in ~45% of Google queries — this is not a future problem, it is a present one.

AI Extractability Checklist

✗
Clear product/business definition in first paragraph
Homepage copy leads with "Join the family" — no definition of what the business sells or where it's located. AI systems can't extract a clean answer to "What does Jeff Hill Trailer Sales sell?"
✗
Self-contained answer blocks for key queries
No content structured as extractable answers. No definition blocks, no how-to formats, no comparison tables anywhere on the site.
✗
Statistics or data with sources cited
Zero data points, no statistics, no cited sources on any page. Statistics boost AI citation probability by +37–40% (Princeton GEO study, 2024).
✗
Expert attribution (name + credentials visible)
Jeff Hill's name appears in the About section but without structured credentials. "In the trailer industry since 1996" and "racing since 2012" are present but not formatted as citable authority signals.
✗
FAQ section with natural-language questions
No FAQ section anywhere on the site. FAQs directly match query patterns that AI systems are asked — they are among the highest-citation content formats.
✗
Schema markup (FAQ, LocalBusiness, Product)
Zero schema markup. Content with schema gets 30–40% higher AI visibility. LocalBusiness schema alone would help AI systems accurately describe and cite this business.
✗
Content recently updated (within 6 months)
Sitemap shows website pages last modified January 2025 — 14+ months old. AI systems weight recency heavily when selecting sources.
✗
Comparison tables for product vs. product queries
No comparison content. "Enclosed trailer vs. utility trailer" or "US Cargo vs. Haul About" pages don't exist. Comparison articles generate ~33% of all AI citations.
✓
AI bots allowed in robots.txt
The robots.txt file is wide open — all user agents allowed with no disallow rules. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, and Google-Extended can all crawl the site. This is correct.

Create structured FAQ content for key buyer questions High Impact
FAQs are among the most cited content formats by AI systems because they directly answer natural-language questions. Jeff Hill should publish FAQs covering: "What types of trailers do you sell?", "Do you offer financing?", "What trailer brands do you carry?", "Can I trade in my old trailer?", "Do you ship trailers or is pickup only?"
Implementation
Add an FAQ section to the homepage and contact page. Add FAQPage schema markup. Keep answers to 40–60 words (optimal extraction length for AI snippets).
Add definition-first content blocks to key pages High Impact
Every key page should lead with a single clear paragraph that defines what the page is about in a way AI can extract. For the shop page: "Jeff Hill Trailer Sales carries enclosed cargo trailers, utility trailers, dump trailers, and concession trailers for sale in Modoc, Indiana. We stock brands including US Cargo, Liberty, Haul About, Stealth, and Force, with prices from $2,850 to $14,495."

This content has low competition, positions Jeff's expertise, and can attract national search traffic from the motorsport community — exactly the audience most likely to travel to buy.
Implementation
Create 3–5 blog posts or guide pages on racing-specific trailer topics. Include Jeff's first-person experience, specific specs (interior height, tie-down positions, etc.), and link to relevant inventory. Use Article schema with Jeff as the named author.
