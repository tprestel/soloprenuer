# Brand Voice — Super Laundry Cincinnati

> This document defines how Super Laundry Cincinnati communicates. Use it to calibrate tone, vocabulary, and writing style across all content — website, social media, signage, and marketing materials.

---

## Core Voice Attributes

Based on analysis of Super Laundry's existing website copy and market positioning, the brand voice should be built around these four qualities:

1. **Straightforward and confident** — Super Laundry makes direct claims without hedging. "Cincinnati's Biggest & Best Laundromats" is bold and simple. The voice doesn't over-explain or over-qualify. It states what it is and moves on.

2. **Neighborhood-friendly** — The brand serves working families and everyday residents. The voice should feel like a trusted neighbor, not a corporate entity. Warm but not saccharine. Helpful but not preachy.

3. **Reliable and no-nonsense** — Laundry is a chore, not a lifestyle. The voice acknowledges this reality. It doesn't try to make laundry exciting — it promises to make it easy, fast, and always available. Dependability is the emotional core.

4. **Inclusive and welcoming** — Super Laundry serves diverse Cincinnati neighborhoods. The voice should feel accessible to everyone regardless of background, age, or income level.

**North star positioning statement:**
*Super Laundry Cincinnati is the always-open, always-ready laundromat network that keeps Cincinnati's neighborhoods running clean — day or night, rain or shine.*

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone Adjustment |
|---|---|
| **Website (homepage, location pages)** | Confident, welcoming, clear. Lead with benefits. Short sentences. Assume the visitor needs directions, hours, and services — fast. |
| **Blog / content** | Helpful, practical, conversational. Think "tips from a neighbor who happens to run a laundromat." Avoid jargon. |
| **Social media** | Warm, casual, community-focused. Celebrate the neighborhoods. Share practical laundry tips. Light humor is fine — but never forced. |
| **Email / SMS** | Direct and useful. Get to the point. Promotional messages should lead with the offer, not the setup. |
| **Signage / in-store** | Clear, instructional, friendly. Assume the customer is in a hurry. Use short phrases and large text. |
| **Google Business / review responses** | Grateful, professional, empathetic. Thank every reviewer. Address concerns directly without being defensive. |

## Vocabulary We Avoid

| Avoid | Use Instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Laundromat experience" | "Getting your laundry done" | Nobody goes to a laundromat for an "experience" — they go to get clean clothes |
| "State-of-the-art" | "Modern machines" or just describe the feature | Overused and vague |
| "Revolutionary" / "innovative" | Skip it — just describe what you offer | Laundry isn't revolutionary and claiming it is undermines credibility |
| "Clients" | "Customers" or "neighbors" | Too formal for a neighborhood laundromat |
| "Solutions" | Name the actual service | Corporate buzzword that means nothing |
| "Utilize" | "Use" | Simpler is always better |
| "Premium" / "luxury" | "Clean" / "well-maintained" / "comfortable" | Super Laundry serves everyday needs, not luxury positioning |
| "Disrupting" / "reimagining" | Don't — just say what you do | Tech startup language doesn't fit a laundromat |

## Writing Style Principles

1. **Short sentences, short paragraphs.** The audience is scanning on mobile while managing laundry. Respect their time. If a sentence has a comma, consider making it two sentences.

2. **Lead with what the customer cares about.** Hours, location, services — in that order. Save the brand story for the About page.

3. **Use "you" and "your" liberally.** "Your clothes are in good hands" beats "Our staff handles laundry with care." Make the customer the subject.

4. **Be specific over clever.** "Open 24 hours, 7 days a week" beats "We never sleep." Clarity wins over creativity in a service business.

5. **Acknowledge the reality of laundry.** It's a chore. Don't pretend it's fun. Instead, position Super Laundry as the place that makes the chore as painless as possible.

6. **Name the neighborhoods.** Every piece of content should feel local. "Our Westwood location on Harrison Ave" is more useful and trustworthy than "our convenient locations."

7. **One call-to-action per context.** Don't ask the customer to follow on social, sign up for emails, and visit the nearest location all at once. Pick one.

## Headline Style

### Patterns that work for Super Laundry:
- **Benefit + location:** "24/7 Laundry in North College Hill — No Quarters Needed"
- **Simple claim:** "Cincinnati's 6-Location Laundromat Network"
- **Customer-centric:** "Your Neighborhood Laundromat, Open When You Need It"
- **Direct and practical:** "Drop Off Your Laundry. Pick It Up Clean."

### What Super Laundry headlines should NOT do:
- Use puns about laundry (e.g., "We're on a roll!" or "Loads of fun!") — unless tested and proven with the audience
- Make vague claims without specifics ("The Best Laundry Experience")
- Use all caps for emphasis (let the words do the work)
- Lead with the company name ("Super Laundry Is Cincinnati's Best") — lead with the customer's need instead

## Copy Examples — Would vs. Wouldn't

### Homepage hero section

**Would write:**
> Clean clothes, any time of day. Super Laundry has 6 locations across Cincinnati with 100+ washers and dryers each. Pay your way — tap, card, or cash. Load $20, get a dollar free. No quarters needed. No waiting. Just clean laundry.

**Wouldn't write:**
> Welcome to Super Laundry Cincinnati! We are proud to be the premier laundry solution provider in the Greater Cincinnati metropolitan area. With our state-of-the-art facilities and commitment to excellence, we deliver an unparalleled laundry experience for all of our valued clients.

### Location page intro

**Would write:**
> Our Avondale location on Reading Rd is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Smart card machines — no quarters needed. Drop off your laundry and we'll have it washed, dried, and folded for you.

**Wouldn't write:**
> The Avondale branch of Super Laundry Cincinnati offers a wide array of laundry solutions designed to meet the diverse needs of our community stakeholders. We leverage cutting-edge smart card technology to provide a seamless customer journey.

## Voice for Specific Audiences

### Families
Emphasize convenience and capacity. "Big machines for big families." Practical, supportive tone. Acknowledge the reality of managing household laundry for multiple people.

### Shift workers / non-traditional hours
Emphasize 24/7 access without judgment. "We're open when you get off work — no matter when that is." Respectful, no-fuss tone.

### First-time laundromat users
Reassuring and instructional. "Never used a laundromat before? Here's how it works." Remove intimidation. Explain the smart card system clearly.

### Drop-off customers
Emphasize time savings and trust. "Drop it off dirty, pick it up fresh." Professional, efficient tone. These customers are paying for convenience — respect that by being direct.
