## Laundromat Team Member

**Status:** Active
**Types:** Full-time · Part-time · Overnight
**Locations:** All 6 Cincinnati locations (Springdale, Avondale, Bond Hill, Westwood, North College Hill, Allison Road)

### About the Role

As a Laundromat Team Member, you'll be the heart of your location — helping customers navigate our self-service machines, assisting with wash-dry-fold drop-offs, keeping the facility clean and welcoming, and being a friendly, familiar face in the neighborhood. No two days are exactly alike, and the work you do genuinely matters to the people you serve.

### What We're Looking For

- Dependable and punctual
- Comfortable working independently and with a team
- Good with people — friendly, approachable
- No prior laundry experience required

### How to Apply

No resume required. Applicants submit their name, email, preferred phone (optional), and which location(s) they're interested in via the careers page form.

**Apply at:** `pages/careers.html`
