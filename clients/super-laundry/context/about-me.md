## What We Do

Super Laundry Cincinnati operates large-format laundromat facilities with 100+ washers and dryers at each location, providing self-service laundry, drop-off wash-dry-fold, and pickup/delivery services to residential customers across Cincinnati.

### Core services:

1. **Self-service laundry** — 100+ washers and dryers at each location. Large-capacity washers ranging from 2-load to 8-load sizes. Folding tables, carts, soaps, snacks, seating, and friendly attendants on-site to assist.
2. **Drop-off wash-dry-fold** — Customers drop off daily wear clothes and pick them up washed, dried, and folded. Shortens laundry day significantly.
3. **Pickup and delivery** — Currently available at the Springdale location. Customers can schedule pickup and delivery of wash-dry-fold orders. Other locations coming soon.
4. **24/7 access** — Most locations are open 24/7. Springdale operates 7 AM – 10 PM daily. Check individual locations for specific hours.

## What Problems We Solve

Super Laundry serves customers who need reliable, accessible laundry facilities — primarily residents without in-unit or in-home washers and dryers. The core problems addressed:

- **No washer/dryer at home:** Many Cincinnati apartment and rental residents lack in-unit laundry. Super Laundry provides a clean, convenient alternative.
- **Large loads and specialty items:** Customers with comforters, bedding, or oversized items that won't fit in home machines.
- **Time constraints:** The drop-off service lets busy customers hand off laundry and pick it up done.
- **24/7 scheduling needs:** Shift workers, parents, and others who need laundry access outside standard business hours benefit from round-the-clock availability.
- **Affordability:** Self-service laundry is significantly cheaper than dry cleaning or delivery laundry services.

## Who We Serve

### Primary customer segments:

1. **Apartment/rental residents without in-unit laundry** — The core customer base. These are individuals and families in Cincinnati neighborhoods who rely on laundromats as their primary laundry solution. Skews toward working-class and middle-income households.

2. **Families with large loads** — Parents managing laundry for multiple household members who benefit from large-capacity commercial machines that process loads faster than home units.

3. **Shift workers and non-traditional schedules** — Nurses, restaurant workers, warehouse employees, and others who need 24/7 access because their schedules don't align with standard business hours.

4. **Students** — College and university students in the Cincinnati area who need affordable laundry options near campus or home.

5. **Drop-off customers** — Time-pressed individuals willing to pay a premium for wash-dry-fold service rather than waiting through the self-service process.

## Market Position

Super Laundry Cincinnati is one of the larger independent laundromat operators in the Cincinnati metro area, competing against a mix of single-location independents and a small number of multi-location or franchise chains. The brand leads on location count (6 locations) and 24/7 accessibility.

The market is primarily local and fragmented — most competitors operate one or two locations. Notable multi-location competitors include WaveMAX Laundry (franchise, Colerain Ave), Bulldog Laundromat (Winton Rd), and Laundry Station Express (northern suburbs). LaundroMax has also entered the Cincinnati market with a large-format location on Glenway Ave.

Super Laundry's current website (superlaundry24.com) is outdated and significantly underperforms compared to competitor sites, representing both a weakness and a major improvement opportunity.

## How We Differ

1. **Most locations in Cincinnati** — 6 locations gives Super Laundry the widest geographic coverage of any single laundromat brand in the metro area.
2. **100+ machines per location** — Not a small neighborhood laundromat. Each location is a full-scale facility with 100+ washers and dryers, including large-capacity machines up to 8-load size.
3. **True 24/7 access (most locations)** — Most locations are open around the clock. Many competitors close by 10 PM or midnight.
4. **Neighborhood-embedded** — Locations are in residential neighborhoods (Avondale, Bond Hill, Westwood, North College Hill, Springdale, Allison St), not commercial strips, making them walkable or a short drive for local residents.
5. **Every way to pay (CASH-CARD-TAP)** — Tap, Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit/debit, smart card, online, and cash. No competitor offers this breadth of payment options. Eliminates the quarters-only friction point.
6. **Rewards program** — Load $20, get a free dollar. Springdale location offers $3 back on $30. Builds loyalty and repeat visits.
7. **Pickup and delivery** — Currently at Springdale, expanding to other locations. Adds a convenience tier that most local competitors don't offer.
8. **Drop-off wash-dry-fold** — Full-service option at all locations for time-pressed customers.
9. **Free parking at the door** — Customers don't have to carry laundry across a parking lot. Small detail, big convenience.

## Community Involvement

**Laundry Love Cincinnati Partnership:**
Super Laundry Westwood (2455 Harrison Ave) hosts a monthly free laundry event through Laundry Love Cincinnati, a nonprofit that provides free laundry services to families and individuals facing financial hardship.

- **Schedule:** 2nd Wednesday of every month, 6–8 PM
- **Location:** Super Laundry Westwood, 2455 Harrison Ave, Cincinnati, OH 45211
- **Details:** Individuals may wash up to 2 loads, families up to 4 loads. First come, first served. All laundry costs and supplies covered through donations. Volunteers and donors welcome.
- **Website:** laundrylovecinci.org/howitworks

This partnership is a key brand story — it demonstrates community investment and should be featured on the homepage, the Westwood location page, and the news/events section.
