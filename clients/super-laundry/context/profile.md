## Audit Findings
- **Website Score:** 3/10 — Squarespace site with zero H1 tags, near-empty LocalBusiness schema (no address/phone/hours), COVID-era hero copy still live, only 3 nav items, and 2 of 6 locations missing from all content
- **GBP Score:** 5/10 — All listings claimed with correct Laundromat category and solid combined review counts (~1,700+), but four different naming formats across 6 listings undermines brand entity recognition
- **SEO Score:** 2/10 — Domain rank 705,461, only 284 organic keywords, invisible for all local intent terms, ranking #63 for highest-value keyword ("24 hour laundry near me")
- **AI Search Score:** 1/10 — Squarespace default robots.txt blocks every major AI crawler (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Google-Extended) — completely walled off from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews
- **Social Score:** 3/10 — Facebook page has 130 followers, Yahoo email address, COVID-era posts still visible, no Instagram found

**Business Context:**
Super Laundry Cincinnati operates 6 laundromat locations across the city. Each has 100+ commercial washers and dryers (2–8 load capacity). Four locations are 24/7; Springdale is 7 AM–10 PM; Allison St is 6 AM–11 PM and is the only location with coin-operated machines (accepts quarters in addition to Cash · Card · Tap — same payment breadth as all other locations). Drop-off wash-dry-fold is available at all staffed locations; pickup & delivery at Springdale only (expanding). The business rebranded from "Clean Living Laundry" — ZoomInfo and data aggregators still show the old name.

**SEO Baseline:**
- Domain rank: 705,461 | Organic keywords: 284 | Est. monthly traffic: ~1,794
- Nearly all traffic from brand terms ("super laundry" #1, "super laundromat" #11)
- Ranking #63 for "24 hour laundry near me" (6,600/mo) — effectively invisible
- Not ranking for "laundromat cincinnati," "wash and fold near me," or any local intent term

**Key Gaps:**
1. **AI search is completely walled off** — The Squarespace default robots.txt blocks GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, and Google-Extended. Anyone asking an AI assistant for a Cincinnati laundromat cannot be recommended Super Laundry. This also affects Google AI Overviews. It's a platform default they likely don't know is on.
2. **Google can't read who they are** — Zero H1 tags sitewide, LocalBusiness schema with no address/phone/hours, and COVID-era hero copy signal a neglected, uncrawlable site. With 6 locations and 3 services, they're invisible for every search that isn't their brand name.
3. **GBP identity is fragmented** — Four different naming formats across 6 listings ("Super Laundry," "Super Laundry-Avondale," "Super Laundry North College Hill," "Westwood Super Laundry") mean Google doesn't recognize them as a unified chain. Two locations share the exact same name "Super Laundry" with no neighborhood qualifier.
4. **Live site is years out of date** — Production site references "Coronavirus concerns" in the hero, lists only 4 of 6 locations, uses outdated "smart card" language, and has a 3-item nav. Customers landing from Google get an incomplete and dated impression of the business.

**Quick Win:**
Remove the AI bot blocks in Squarespace (Settings → SEO → Crawlers) — uncheck the blocks on GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, and Google-Extended. This is a one-time 5-minute settings change that immediately opens Super Laundry to AI search visibility. It must also be a day-one configuration requirement on the HubSpot rebuild before launch.
