## Their Story

Super Laundry Cincinnati is a locally owned multi-location laundromat chain — 6 locations across Cincinnati's neighborhoods. Positions itself as "Cincinnati's Biggest & Best Laundromats." One of the largest independent laundromat operators in the Cincinnati metro.

The business rebranded from "Clean Living Laundry" — ZoomInfo and data aggregators still show the old name. This rebrand should not be referenced in client-facing copy.

**Community angle:** The Westwood location (2455 Harrison Ave) and Avondale location (3545 Reading Rd) both host a monthly free laundry event through Laundry Love Cincinnati — a nonprofit providing free laundry to families facing financial hardship. 2nd Wednesday of every month, 6–8 PM. Individuals: up to 2 loads. Families: up to 4 loads. This is a genuine community story — feature it on the homepage and both location pages.

**Award:** BusinessRate Best of 2025 — Best Laundromat in Cincinnati (45229 area). Based on verified Google Reviews data across 45,229 businesses. Earned, not application-based. Award graphic is being purchased by the owner — not yet received.

## What They Do (In Their Words)

> "Self-service laundry, drop-off wash-dry-fold, and pickup and delivery — across 6 locations in Cincinnati."

**Core services:**
- **Self-service laundry** — 100+ washers and dryers at each location. Large-capacity machines from 2-load to 8-load. Folding tables, carts, soaps, snacks, seating, attendants on-site.
- **Drop-off wash-dry-fold** — Customers drop off daily wear, pick up washed, dried, and folded. Available at all staffed locations.
- **Pickup and delivery** — Currently Springdale only. Expanding to other locations.
- **24/7 access** — Most locations open around the clock. Springdale: 7 AM–10 PM daily. Allison St: 6 AM–11 PM daily. Cash · Card · Tap + coin-operated (only location that also accepts coins/quarters).

**Payment (CASH-CARD-TAP):**
Tap to pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit/debit card, smart card (loyalty), online payment, cash, mobile app. No competitor matches this breadth — it eliminates the quarters-only friction point entirely.

**Rewards:**
- All locations: Load $20, get a FREE dollar
- Springdale: Spend $30, earn $3 reward
- Mobile app with rewards integration

## Who They Serve (Their Perspective)

1. **Apartment/rental residents without in-unit laundry** — Core customer. Working-class and middle-income Cincinnati households who rely on laundromats as their primary option.
2. **Families with large loads** — Parents managing laundry for multiple people. Large-capacity commercial machines process loads faster than home units.
3. **Shift workers and non-traditional schedules** — Nurses, restaurant workers, warehouse employees. They need 24/7 because standard hours don't work.
4. **Students** — Affordable options near campus or home.
5. **Drop-off customers** — Time-pressed individuals who pay for wash-dry-fold rather than waiting through self-service. Convenience is the sale here.

**Voice note:** Use "customers" or "neighbors" — never "clients." Too formal for a neighborhood laundromat.

## What Makes Them Different (Their Words)

1. **Most locations in Cincinnati** — 6 locations, widest geographic coverage of any single laundromat brand in the metro.
2. **100+ machines per location** — Not a small neighborhood laundromat. Full-scale facilities with large-capacity machines up to 8-load size.
3. **True 24/7 access** — Most locations open around the clock. Many competitors close by 10 PM or midnight.
4. **Neighborhood-embedded** — Locations are in residential neighborhoods (Avondale, Bond Hill, Westwood, North College Hill, Springdale, Allison St), not commercial strips. Walkable for local residents.
5. **Every way to pay (CASH-CARD-TAP)** — Tap, Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit/debit, smart card, online, cash. No competitor matches this.
6. **Rewards program** — Load $20, get a free dollar. Springdale: $3 back on $30.
7. **Pickup and delivery** — Currently Springdale. Expanding. Most local competitors don't offer this.
8. **Drop-off wash-dry-fold** — Full-service option at all locations.
9. **Free parking at the door** — Customers don't carry laundry across a parking lot.
10. **Locally owned, not a franchise** — Not part of a national chain.

## Brand Voice (Confirmed)

Drawn from `brand-voice.md` — treat this as confirmed.

**The four voice attributes:**
1. **Straightforward and confident** — Bold, simple, no hedging. "Cincinnati's Biggest & Best Laundromats" is the model.
2. **Neighborhood-friendly** — Trusted neighbor energy, not corporate. Warm but not saccharine.
3. **Reliable and no-nonsense** — Laundry is a chore. Don't pretend it's fun. Promise to make it easy, fast, always available.
4. **Inclusive and welcoming** — Accessible to everyone regardless of background, age, or income.

**North star positioning:**
> *Super Laundry Cincinnati is the always-open, always-ready laundromat network that keeps Cincinnati's neighborhoods running clean — day or night, rain or shine.*

**Phrases to use:**
- Wash-dry-fold (not "laundry service" as a generic)
- CASH-CARD-TAP — display rendering in UI and copy: **Cash · Card · Tap** (middle dots, title case, not dashes or all-caps)
- 24/7, always open, open around the clock
- 100+ washers and dryers
- Large-capacity machines, 2-load to 8-load
- Shorten your laundry day
- No quarters needed, pay your way
- Free parking at the door
- Load $20, get a dollar free
- Neighborhood, your neighborhood
- Location names: Springdale, Avondale, Bond Hill, North College Hill, Westwood, Allison St

**Phrases/tones to avoid:**
- "Laundromat experience" → say "getting your laundry done"
- "State-of-the-art" → describe the actual feature
- "Revolutionary" / "innovative"
- "Clients" → "customers" or "neighbors"
- "Solutions" → name the actual service
- "Premium" / "luxury" → "clean" / "well-maintained" / "comfortable"
- Puns about laundry
- Vague claims without specifics
- Leading with the company name — lead with the customer's need

**Writing style:**
- Short sentences, short paragraphs — audience is on mobile
- Lead with what the customer cares about: hours, location, services
- Use "you" and "your" liberally
- Specific over clever — "Open 24 hours, 7 days a week" beats "We never sleep"
- Name the neighborhoods in every piece of content
- One call-to-action per context
