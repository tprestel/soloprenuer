## Business Context
Super Laundry Cincinnati operates 6 laundromat locations across Cincinnati, making it one of the largest independent laundromat chains in the market. Each location has 100+ commercial washers and dryers (2-load to 8-load capacity) — large enough for comforters, rugs, and full family weekly loads. The core model is straightforward: show up, wash, go. But they've layered services on top — drop-off wash-dry-fold at all staffed locations and pickup & delivery at Springdale (expanding). Four locations are 24/7; two have extended-but-not-overnight hours. The Allison St location is the outlier — coin-operated and likely the oldest in the fleet. The business recently rebranded from "Clean Living Laundry" to "Super Laundry Cincinnati" — ZoomInfo and data aggregators still show the old name.

## Where They Operate
Six physical locations across Cincinnati, OH:
- **Springdale** — 11627 Springfield Pike, Springdale OH 45246 | 7 AM–10 PM daily | Pickup & delivery available | Rewards program ($3 back/$30)
- **Avondale** — 3545 Reading Rd, Cincinnati OH 45229 | Open 24/7 | Laundry Love host (2nd Wed/mo)
- **Bond Hill** — 4368 Reading Rd, Cincinnati OH 45229 | Open 24/7
- **Westwood** — 2455 Harrison Ave, Cincinnati OH 45211 | Open 24/7 | Laundry Love host (2nd Wed/mo)
- **North College Hill** — 1585 Goodman Ave, Cincinnati OH 45224 | Open 24/7
- **Allison St** — 4522 Allison St, Cincinnati OH 45212 | Hours 6 AM–11 PM | Cash · Card · Tap + coin-operated (only location that also accepts coins/quarters) | No phone listed

Service geography: primarily west and northwest Cincinnati neighborhoods plus Springdale (suburban northwest). No east-side or Norwood/Blue Ash presence.

## Who They Serve
- **24/7 seekers** — shift workers, people with unpredictable schedules, anyone locked out of their building laundry room
- **Large load customers** — families needing 8-load machines for comforters/bedding that don't fit in home washers
- **Convenience-first professionals** — drop-off and pickup/delivery users who want clean laundry without touching it
- **Price-sensitive community members** — affordable, no-subscription pricing; Laundry Love free wash program for those in need
- **Geographic capture** — residents in Avondale, Bond Hill, Westwood, NCH who lack nearby alternatives

## What Makes Them Different
- **Scale** — 100+ machines per location is a significant operational advantage; customers rarely wait for a machine
- **24/7 access** at 4 of 6 locations — a real differentiator vs. smaller independents with limited hours
- **Pickup & delivery** at Springdale — premium service with high-margin potential; expanding
- **Laundry Love partnership** (Westwood + Avondale) — community goodwill program that creates loyalty and press coverage opportunities
- **No-quarters model** — 5 of 6 locations use card/tap payment; removes friction for younger customers
- **Rewards program** — Springdale $3-back-on-$30 earn mechanic tied to the app
- **Best of 2025 award** — Named Best Laundromat in Cincinnati by BusinessRate (based on verified Google Reviews across 45,229 businesses)

## Common Misconceptions
- **"They're the same as a regular coin laundry"** — The brand pushes modern card payment and tech forward amenities, but the Allison St coin-op location could confuse new customers about the overall brand
- **"Clean Living Laundry"** — ZoomInfo and data aggregators still show the old name; potential confusion for customers who find old listings
- **"Pickup & delivery is everywhere"** — It's only at Springdale; the site makes this clear but customers expecting it at other locations may be surprised
- **"Only the laundromat in their neighborhood"** — Many customers won't know Super Laundry has 6 locations; each GBP listing functions as a separate brand island rather than a unified chain impression

## Brand Voice (Inferred)
- **Tone:** Warm and direct — community-oriented, practical, not aspirational. The copy says "Laundry Day never easier" not "Transform your laundry routine."
- **Energy:** Friendly and accessible, not premium or edgy. Appeals to families and neighborhood regulars.
- **Phrases they use naturally:** "Cincinnati's Biggest & Best Laundromats," "clean and fresh," "every way to pay," "friendly attendants," "right at the door"
- **Phrases/language to avoid:** Luxury positioning, tech-forward jargon, anything that sounds like a startup. This is a neighborhood utility business.

### Gaps Super Laundry Could Own
- **Pickup & delivery SEO** — Neither City Limits nor Laundromax appear to be targeting "laundry pickup and delivery near me" (1,300/mo, $4.70 CPC) aggressively. Super Laundry has the service; it just needs the content.
- **Neighborhood SEO pages** — Individual location pages with rich FAQ schema (already built in staging) can own hyper-local searches (e.g., "laundromat avondale," "laundromat westwood") where GBP-only competitors have no web content
- **Drop-off as hero service** — City Limits doesn't feature drop-off prominently; Super Laundry can own "wash and fold near me" (14,800/mo) if the service pages are optimized
- **Unified brand story** — Both major competitors have less community narrative than Super Laundry (Laundry Love, rewards, 24/7 community spaces); this could anchor content and PR

## Opportunity Summary
Super Laundry Cincinnati has real operational scale — 6 locations, 24/7 access, 100+ machines each, drop-off service, and pickup & delivery — but their digital presence doesn't reflect any of it. Their website generates ~1,800 visits/month, almost entirely from people already searching for them by name. The near-me search opportunity ("wash and fold near me," "24 hour laundry near me," "drop off laundry near me") totals 25,000+ monthly searches that they're invisible for. With a full website rebuild in progress on HubSpot and location pages already structured with FAQ schema, the technical foundation is being laid — the opportunity is to make sure the SEO signals, GBP optimization, and content hierarchy are in place when the site goes live so it can actually start capturing local search traffic from day one.
