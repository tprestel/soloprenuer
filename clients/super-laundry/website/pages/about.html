Our Story

A laundromat network built for Cincinnati's neighborhoods

Super Laundry is locally owned — not a franchise, not a chain. Every location is part of Cincinnati, placed where people actually live and work: Springdale, Avondale, Bond Hill, Westwood, North College Hill, and Allison Street.

We built this network for working families, shift workers, and anyone who needs clean laundry without a complicated process. Big machines, flexible hours, every way to pay. Most locations are open around the clock because laundry doesn't wait for business hours.

When your neighbors choose us, we take that seriously. That's why we invest in the communities we're in — from keeping our facilities clean and well-maintained to hosting events like Laundry Love that give back to families who need it most.

Community

Giving back to the neighbors who need it most

Our Westwood and Avondale locations host Laundry Love Cincinnati every month — a nonprofit event that provides free laundry to families facing financial hardship. Clean clothes aren't a luxury. They're a foundation for getting to work, keeping kids in school, and holding a household together.

We're proud to open our doors for this. No cost, no judgment, just clean laundry for the people who need it.
