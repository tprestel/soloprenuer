How It Works

Self-Service in Three Steps

Walk in. Pick your machines. Load and pay. That's all there is to it. No account, no appointment, no waiting on anyone else.

1

Choose Your Machines

Pick the washer size that fits your load — 2-load for everyday items, up to 8-load for comforters, bedding, and full family hauls. With 100+ machines per location, you won't be waiting.

2

Load and Pay

Load your laundry, add detergent, and pay directly on the machine. Card, tap, Apple Pay, Google Pay, smart card, and cash all accepted at most locations. No quarters needed at 5 of 6 locations.

3

Come Back to Collect

Wait in-store or run an errand and come back. Folding tables and carts are available at every location. Most full wash-and-dry runs are done in about an hour.

Machine Capacity

The Right Machine for Every Load

Every Super Laundry location has a full range of washer sizes. Whether you're doing a quick load of daily wear or tackling a king comforter that hasn't fit in your home machine in years — we have the capacity for it.

With 100+ machines at each location, large-capacity washers are nearly always available. No circling the floor waiting for the one big machine to open up.

2×

2-Load — Everyday Wear

Shirts, pants, underwear, socks, and everyday items. Fastest cycle. Ideal for smaller households or in-between loads.

3×

3-Load — Medium Household

A full week's laundry for one or two people. Towels, jeans, and mixed loads. Handles more volume per cycle to save time.

4×

4-Load — Larger Families

Multiple people's clothing in a single load. Great for sorting out a full weekend of laundry in fewer trips.

6–8×

6-Load & 8-Load — Bulky Items

Comforters, king bedding, area rugs, sleeping bags, and moving day loads. These won't fit in a home washer — our big machines handle them with no problem.

Common questions about self-service

No account required. Walk in, pick an open machine, load your laundry, and pay directly on the machine. No app needed — though the Super Laundry app is available if you want to track rewards. Most locations accept card, tap, Apple Pay, Google Pay, smart card, and cash right on the washer.

Every location has machines from 2-load capacity (everyday clothing) up to 8-load capacity (comforters, rugs, king bedding, and full family loads). With 100+ machines at each location, large-capacity washers are almost always available — no waiting around for the one big machine to open up.

Five of six locations (Springdale, Avondale, Bond Hill, Westwood, North College Hill) accept card, tap to pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, smart card, mobile app, and cash. The Allison Street location is coin-operated — bring quarters. Cash, card, and tap are also accepted there.
