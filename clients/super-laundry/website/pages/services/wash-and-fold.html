How It Works

Three Steps to Clean Laundry

Shorten your laundry day down to two quick trips. We handle everything in between.

1

Drop It Off

Bring your laundry to any staffed location in a bag or basket. Walk in anytime during staffed hours — no appointment, no scheduling. An attendant will check you in and give you an estimated pickup time.

2

We Handle Everything

Our team washes, dries, and folds your laundry using commercial-grade machines. We sort by load size, use the right water temperature, and handle your clothes with care from start to finish.

3

Pick Up Clean

Come back when your laundry is ready. Everything is neatly folded and waiting for you. Pay at pickup — no prepayment required. CASH · CARD · TAP accepted at all locations.
