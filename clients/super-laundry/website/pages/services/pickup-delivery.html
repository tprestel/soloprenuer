How It Works

Three Steps. Zero Laundry Time.

Call to schedule, we pick up from your door, and your clothes come back clean. That's it.

1

Schedule Your Pickup

Call us at (513) 376-7129 or log in to your account at mylaundryorder.com to set up your pickup. We'll confirm your address, agree on a time, and answer any questions before we arrive.

2

We Wash & Fold

We bring your laundry to our Springdale facility and run it through our commercial washers and dryers. Everything gets washed, fully dried, and neatly folded — handled with care from start to finish.

3

Delivered Back Clean

Your clean, folded laundry is delivered back to your door. Ready to put straight into the drawer. No sorting, no waiting, no trips to the laundromat.
