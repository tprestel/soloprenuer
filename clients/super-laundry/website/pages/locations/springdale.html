Questions

Frequently asked about Springdale

Answers to what customers ask most before visiting.

What are the hours at Springdale?
Monday–Thursday we're open 7 AM to 10 PM. On weekends, we stay open continuously — from Friday at 7 AM straight through to Sunday at 10 PM, no overnight closing. Attendants are on-site during all open hours.
Do you offer pickup and delivery in Springdale?
Yes — Springdale is currently the only Super Laundry location offering laundry pickup and delivery. Call (513) 376-7129 to schedule your first pickup.
What's the largest machine size at Springdale?
We have machines from 2-load up to 8-load capacity. The 8-load machines handle comforters, rugs, and full family loads easily.
Is there parking at this location?
Yes — free parking right at the door. You don't have to carry laundry across a parking lot.
How does the Springdale rewards program work?
At Springdale, spend $30 and get $3 back — automatically added to your balance every time. Use the app to track your balance and pay on any machine.
What payment methods does Springdale accept?
CASH-CARD-TAP. We accept cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, tap to pay, smart card, and mobile app. No quarters needed.
