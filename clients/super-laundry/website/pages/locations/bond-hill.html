Questions

Frequently asked about Bond Hill

Answers to what customers ask most before visiting.

What are the hours at Bond Hill?
Super Laundry Bond Hill is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including every weekend and holiday. The doors never close.
Can I drop off laundry at Bond Hill?
Yes. Wash-dry-fold drop-off is available during staffed hours. Bring your laundry in, an attendant will check you in, weigh your items, and give you a pickup time. No appointment needed.
What's the largest machine size at Bond Hill?
We have machines from 2-load up to 8-load capacity. The large machines handle comforters, rugs, and full family loads with ease.
Is there parking at Bond Hill?
Yes — free parking is available right at the door on Reading Road. No carrying laundry across a parking lot.
How does the rewards program work at Bond Hill?
Load $20 onto your smart card and get a free dollar — available at every Super Laundry location including Bond Hill. Use the mobile app to track your balance and pay on any machine.
What payment methods does Bond Hill accept?
CASH-CARD-TAP. We accept cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, tap to pay, smart card, and mobile app. No quarters needed.
