Questions

Frequently asked about Westwood

Answers to what customers ask most before visiting.

What are the hours at Super Laundry Westwood?
Super Laundry Westwood is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including weekends and holidays. The doors never close.
Does Westwood host Laundry Love?
Yes. Westwood hosts Laundry Love Cincinnati every 2nd Wednesday of the month from 6 to 8 PM. We provide free machines for families facing financial hardship — up to 2 loads per individual, 4 loads per family. No questions asked.
Does Westwood offer wash-dry-fold drop-off?
Yes. Drop off your laundry during staffed hours and an attendant will check you in, weigh your items, and give you a pickup time. No appointment needed.
Is there parking at this location?
Yes — free parking right at the door, available any time of day or night. Easy in, easy out.
What machine sizes are available at Westwood?
Westwood has 100+ commercial machines in a range of sizes — from 2-load up to 8-load capacity. The large machines handle comforters, rugs, and full family loads with ease.
What payment methods does Westwood accept?
CASH-CARD-TAP. We accept cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, tap to pay, smart card, and mobile app. No quarters needed.
