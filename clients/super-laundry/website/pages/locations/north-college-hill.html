Questions

Frequently asked about North College Hill

Answers to what customers ask most before visiting.

Is Super Laundry North College Hill open 24 hours?
Yes — North College Hill is open 24 hours a day, every day of the week. That includes weekends and holidays. Machines are always available.
Does North College Hill offer wash-dry-fold drop-off?
Yes. Wash-dry-fold drop-off is available during staffed hours. Bring your laundry in, an attendant will weigh it and give you a pickup time. No appointment needed.
How many machines are available at this location?
North College Hill has 100+ commercial washers and dryers in sizes from 2-load to 8-load capacity. The larger machines handle comforters, blankets, and full family loads.
Is there parking at North College Hill?
Yes — free parking is available right at the door. Easy access at any hour of the day or night.
What payment methods does North College Hill accept?
CASH-CARD-TAP. We accept cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, tap to pay, smart card, and mobile app. No quarters needed.
Does North College Hill offer laundry pickup and delivery?
Not at this location. Pickup and delivery is currently available at our Springdale location. Call (513) 376-7129 or visit the Springdale page to learn more.
