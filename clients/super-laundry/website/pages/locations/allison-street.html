Questions

Frequently asked about Allison Street

Answers to what customers ask most before visiting.

What are the hours at Allison Street?
Open every day 6 AM to 11 PM — including weekends and holidays.
Is Allison Street coin-operated?
Yes. Allison Street is our traditional coin-operated location. Bring quarters — no card reader, no app, no smart card. Just quarters and you're set.
Do you offer drop-off or pickup and delivery here?
No — Allison Street is self-service only. For wash-dry-fold drop-off or laundry pickup and delivery, visit our Springdale location at 11627 Springfield Pike.
What payment is accepted at Allison Street?
Cash and coins only. Card readers, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and tap to pay are not available at this location. There is a coin machine on-site to break bills into quarters.
What machine sizes are at Allison Street?
We have washers and dryers from 2-load up to 8-load capacity. The larger machines handle comforters, rugs, and big family loads without a problem.
Is there parking at Allison Street?
Yes — free parking right at the door. Easy in, easy out.
