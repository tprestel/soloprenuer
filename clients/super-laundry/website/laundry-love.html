About the Event

What is Laundry Love Cincinnati?

Laundry Love is a national nonprofit network with a straightforward mission — provide free, dignified laundry access to people who can't afford it. No application, no paperwork, no gatekeeping. Just open doors and clean machines.

The Cincinnati chapter partners with local laundromats to host regular free laundry events. Super Laundry's Westwood and Avondale locations are both partners. On the second Wednesday of every month, we open our machines, supply the detergent, and make sure everyone who walks in is taken care of.

This isn't a one-time thing. It happens every month, same time, same place — at two locations across Cincinnati. Neighbors who need it can count on it.

Why We Do This

Clean clothes shouldn't be a barrier.

We opened our Westwood location to this community years ago. Hosting Laundry Love is an extension of that.

Clean laundry is fundamental. It affects whether kids can go to school, whether adults can show up to work, whether families feel dignity in their daily lives. A load of laundry is a small thing. What it makes possible isn't.

We're proud to open our doors for it. Every month. No conditions.

About the Organization

Laundry Love Cincinnati is part of a national movement.

Laundry Love partners with local laundromats across the United States to host regular free laundry events for people in need. There are chapters in dozens of cities — each one run through local partnerships with businesses willing to open their doors.

Cincinnati is one of those chapters. Super Laundry Westwood is one of those partners.
