FAQ

Common Questions

Most Super Laundry locations are open 24/7. Springdale is open 7 AM – 10 PM daily. Allison St is open 6 AM – 11 PM daily. Check your nearest location for exact hours.
No quarters needed at most locations. We accept tap-to-pay (Apple Pay, Google Pay), credit/debit card, smart card, online payment, mobile app, and cash. The Allison St location is coin-operated.
Bring your laundry to any staffed location, drop it off with an attendant, and come back to pick it up washed, dried, and folded. Turnaround time varies by location — ask the attendant when you drop off.
Pickup and delivery is currently available from our Springdale location and expanding to additional locations. Contact us at (513) 873-5939 to schedule a pickup or check availability in your area.
Every location has 100+ commercial washers and dryers with machines ranging from 2-load to 8-load capacity. The large-capacity machines are great for comforters, blankets, and bulky items.
Yes. Every Super Laundry location has free dedicated parking right at the door. You won't need to carry laundry across a lot.
Load $20 onto your account at any location and get a free dollar added automatically. At Springdale, spend $30 and earn $3 back. Download our mobile app to manage your rewards and load funds from anywhere.
Wash-dry-fold drop-off is available at all staffed locations — Springdale, Avondale, Bond Hill, Westwood, and North College Hill. The Allison St location is self-service and coin-operated.
