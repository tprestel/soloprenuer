## Ongoing GBP — Do Monthly for All Locations

### GBP Posts
Post at least **2x per month** per listing. Rotate through these types:

**What's New post (monthly):**
Use to announce hours changes, new services, or updates. Example:
> "Springdale is now open all weekend — continuously from Friday 7 AM through Sunday 10 PM. No overnight closing. Come in any time."

**Offer post (monthly):**
Use to highlight the rewards program or a specific service. Example:
> "Spend $30 at Springdale, earn $3 back — automatically, every time. Download the Super Laundry app to track your balance and pay on any machine."

**Event post (Avondale + Westwood only — monthly):**
> "Laundry Love Cincinnati is back at Super Laundry [Westwood / Avondale] — 2nd Wednesday of the month, 6–8 PM. Free laundry for families in need. Individuals: up to 2 loads. Families: up to 4 loads. No sign-up required."
