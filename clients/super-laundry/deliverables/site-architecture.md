## Keyword Research Notes

**Neighborhood-specific terms validated via Semrush (2026-03-26):**
- "laundromat avondale cincinnati" — 0 volume
- "laundromat westwood cincinnati" — 0 volume
- "laundromat bond hill cincinnati" — 0 volume
- "laundromat north college hill cincinnati" — 0 volume
- "laundromat springdale ohio" — 30/mo (marginal, not worth a dedicated page)
- "coin laundry cincinnati" — 30/mo (not worth targeting)

**Conclusion:** Individual location pages cannot be justified on keyword volume alone. They should be built as GBP-support utility pages — structured data, accurate NAP, service/hours info per location — but assigned no dedicated keyword target. They will support "near me" queries through GBP signals + schema, not through organic ranking for named-neighborhood terms.

## Flags for Operator Review

1. **Allison St address confirmed** — 4522 Allison St, Cincinnati OH 45212.
2. **Drop-off and pickup pricing** — Profile.md notes the $1.15/lb figure on the production site is likely outdated (5+ years old). Do NOT publish pricing on wash-and-fold or pickup-delivery pages until confirmed by client.
3. **Location pages: no keyword target** — This is intentional and validated. All 6 neighborhood-specific keywords returned 0 monthly search volume in Semrush. Location pages serve GBP and conversion purposes only.
4. **Pickup & delivery availability** — Currently Springdale only. Confirm with client whether expansion is announced, in progress, or should be omitted from copy.
5. **"Drop-Off" as separate page vs. combined with Wash & Fold** — Drop-off laundry and wash-and-fold are the same service at a laundromat. These have been combined into one page (/wash-and-fold) targeting both keyword sets. If the client uses distinct pricing or turnaround for "drop-off" vs. a premium "wash and fold" tier, this can be split into two pages — but keyword volume does not justify the split on SEO grounds alone.
