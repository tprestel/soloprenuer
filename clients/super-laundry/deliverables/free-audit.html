04 · What We Found

The Three Gaps That Matter

These aren't technical complaints — they're the reasons customers who are looking for you right now aren't finding you.

Finding 01

AI is asking about Cincinnati laundromats — and can't mention you

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview for local recommendations before they ever do a traditional search. When someone asks "best 24-hour laundromat in Cincinnati," Super Laundry doesn't come up — not because you're unknown, but because the current website has actively blocked every AI crawler from reading it. This isn't a content problem. It's a single platform setting.

robots.txt (superlaundry24.com) — Disallow: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, Amazonbot, FacebookBot + 15 more AI crawlers
Finding 02

Google knows your name — but not what you do or where you are

The current website ranks #1 for "super laundry" — your brand name. But for every search that matters ("wash and fold near me," "laundromat open 24 hours," "drop off laundry Cincinnati"), you're essentially invisible. The reason: zero H1 tags across the entire site, a LocalBusiness schema record with no address, no phone number, and no hours, and a homepage that still mentions "Coronavirus concerns" as current news. Google needs clear signals to know what a site is about. Right now, those signals aren't there.

LocalBusiness schema on superlaundry24.com → address: "" (empty) · telephone: (none) · openingHours: (none)
Finding 03

Six locations, four different names — Google doesn't see a chain

When Google's local algorithm evaluates a multi-location business, consistent naming across Google Business Profile listings is a key signal that these are part of the same brand. Right now, your six locations appear under four different name formats — which means Google treats them as loosely related independents rather than a unified 6-location chain. That fragmentation reduces the brand authority that helps individual listings rank higher in local packs.

"Super Laundry" (Bond Hill) · "Super Laundry-Avondale" · "Super Laundry North College Hill"
"Westwood Super Laundry" · "Super Laundry" (Allison — same name as Bond Hill)
