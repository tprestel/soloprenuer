Overview

Based on Our Audit

We ran a full digital presence audit on Super Laundry Cincinnati — website, Google Business Profile, search rankings, and AI search visibility. The findings confirmed what the search data suggests: the operational foundation is strong (6 locations, 24/7 access, 100+ machines, drop-off and pickup services), but the digital presence doesn't reflect any of it. The most urgent issue wasn't outdated copy or a dated website — it was that your Squarespace robots.txt file is actively blocking every major AI crawler, including the ones that power ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity. While competitors can be recommended to anyone asking an AI assistant for a Cincinnati laundromat, Super Laundry can't appear in those results at all. That's a platform default most businesses don't know is on.

This proposal covers the work required to fix the foundational gaps, complete and optimize the HubSpot rebuild currently in progress, and establish a monthly growth program to build rankings and Google Business Profile presence over time.

Audit Findings

What We're Fixing

01
AI search is completely blocked — you can't be recommended by ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews
When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews for a laundromat in Cincinnati, AI assistants pull from websites they're allowed to read. Squarespace's default settings block all of them — and because your HubSpot rebuild needs the right configuration at launch, this must be a day-one requirement before the new site goes live.
robots.txt blocks: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, Google-Extended — all four major AI crawlers
02
Google can't determine what you are, where you are, or what you offer
Your production site has zero H1 tags sitewide — none. The LocalBusiness schema that exists has no address, no phone number, and no hours. Google needs these signals to rank you for searches like "laundromat near me" or "wash and fold Cincinnati." Without them, you're invisible for every search that isn't someone already looking up your name.
H1 tags found: 0 across entire site · LocalBusiness schema: name only, no address/phone/hours
03
Your Google Business Profile listings present as six unrelated businesses, not one chain
Google uses your business name across all listings to build its understanding of your brand. Right now, four different naming formats are in use across six locations — which means Google doesn't recognize Super Laundry as a unified business. Fixing this is a one-time cleanup with compounding long-term benefit as Google builds entity recognition across all six GBPs.
Active formats: "Super Laundry" / "Super Laundry-Avondale" / "Super Laundry North College Hill" / "Westwood Super Laundry"
04
The production site is years out of date — customers are landing on an incomplete, dated impression
Your live site references "Coronavirus concerns" in the hero section, lists only 4 of 6 locations, uses outdated "smart card" language, and has a 3-item navigation. Any customer who finds you through search and lands on the production site gets a version of the business that doesn't exist anymore. The HubSpot rebuild addresses this — but it needs to be the right build from an SEO standpoint.
Hero copy: "Coronavirus concerns" still live · Locations shown: 4 of 6 · Nav items: 3

Why HubSpot matters beyond the website

Every business running on HubSpot knows exactly where their leads come from, has every contact in one place, and never loses a follow-up. Most laundromats at this scale track pickup/delivery requests by phone and text. HubSpot turns that into a pipeline — New Inquiry → Contacted → Confirmed. It's the infrastructure that makes growth trackable.
