04 — On-Page SEO

Title tags, meta descriptions & headings

High 6 title tags exceed 60 characters — will truncate in SERPs
Impact
Google displays approximately 600px (~60 chars) of title text. Longer titles are cut off with "…" reducing click-through rates and keyword visibility.
Pages
/services/self-service (78c)  ·  /services/pickup-delivery (72c)  ·  /locations/north-college-hill (68c)  ·  /locations/springdale (68c)  ·  /locations/locations (66c)  ·  /locations/allison-street (65c)
Fix
Trim titles to 55–60 chars while keeping primary keyword and brand name. Example: "Self-Service Laundry Cincinnati | 100+ Machines" (49c) vs. current 78c title.
High 7 meta descriptions exceed 160 characters — truncated in SERPs
Impact
Meta descriptions over 160 characters are cut off in search results, often mid-sentence. This wastes the opportunity to deliver a complete call to action and reduces CTR.
Pages
/about (193c)  ·  /locations/locations (187c)  ·  /locations/allison-street (172c)  ·  /services/self-service (173c)  ·  /careers (173c)  ·  /services/pickup-delivery (167c)  ·  /contact (163c)
Fix
Trim each to 150–158 chars. Prioritize: lead with the location/service name, include the primary keyword, and end with a clear action ("Open 24/7 — find your nearest location").
Medium Schema type inconsistency — LaundryOrDryCleaning vs Laundry
Impact
The homepage uses LaundryOrDryCleaning (the most specific Schema.org type for laundromats), while all 6 location pages use the less specific Laundry type. Google and AI assistants benefit from the most specific type available.
Fix
Update all 6 location pages to use "@type": "LaundryOrDryCleaning" instead of "@type": "Laundry". This is a single string change per page.
Medium About & Contact pages use Organization schema — should be LocalBusiness
Impact
Organization schema is generic. For a local brick-and-mortar business, LocalBusiness (or the more specific LaundryOrDryCleaning) signals geographic relevance to both Google and AI assistants answering local queries.
Fix
Update /about and /contact to use LocalBusiness schema with full NAP data (name, address, phone, hours). The /contact page already shows all 6 location cards — the schema should reflect this.
Medium No AggregateRating schema — 1,200+ Google reviews going unclaimed
Impact
Super Laundry has 1,200+ verified Google reviews across 6 locations (4.0–4.2★ average). Adding AggregateRating schema to location pages makes these reviews appear as star ratings in SERPs — a significant CTR booster — and gives AI assistants a citation source when answering "best laundromat Cincinnati."
Fix
Add aggregateRating property to each location's JSON-LD schema. Use each location's confirmed GBP rating and review count. Example: Bond Hill 4.0★ / 482 reviews, Avondale 4.2★ / 218 reviews.
Low /news and /blog in sitemap — empty HubSpot default pages
Impact
Google crawls and evaluates all sitemap URLs. Empty pages create thin-content signals and waste crawl budget. Minor issue pre-launch but grows over time.
Fix
Either remove /news and /blog from the HubSpot sitemap, redirect them to the homepage, or add a content plan. A blog strategy would benefit long-term SEO — but launch without content is worse than no blog at all.

06 — AI SEO

Visibility in ChatGPT, Perplexity & Google AI Overviews

The biggest single improvement in this rebuild: The old Squarespace site blocked every AI crawler by default — GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, and Google-Extended were all in robots.txt Disallow. Any AI assistant asked "best laundromat in Cincinnati" literally could not access or reference the site. The new HubSpot build has no AI crawler blocks, which opens the door for the first time.
✅ Fixed
AI crawlers unblocked
robots.txt now allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, and Google-Extended. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews can now crawl and index content for the first time.
✅ Good
FAQPage schema on 9 pages
FAQ schema on the homepage, all 6 location pages, and all 4 service pages gives AI assistants structured Q&A data to pull answers from directly. Covers hours, payment, services, and machine capacity.
✅ Good
Conversational location content
Location pages contain natural-language answers to high-frequency local queries: "What are the hours?", "Do I need quarters?", "Is there parking?". This content is directly citable by AI assistants.
✅ Good
NAP consistent across all pages
Name, address, phone, and hours are consistently structured and schema-marked on every location page. AI assistants can confidently return these details without hallucinating.
⚠️ Missing
No AggregateRating in schema
1,200+ Google reviews across 6 locations are not referenced in any structured data. AI assistants answering "best laundromat Cincinnati" look for rating signals — this is a missed citation opportunity.
⚠️ Opportunity
No AI Overview–optimized content
Queries like "What's the best 24-hour laundromat in Cincinnati?" or "Where can I drop off laundry near me in Cincinnati?" aren't answered anywhere in a format AI assistants can cite directly. A short FAQ or "Why Super Laundry" section on the homepage could capture these.
