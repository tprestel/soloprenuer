Issues Found

5 total · 2 high · 2 medium · 1 low
High
Meta description is 197 chars — Google truncates at ~160
The current meta is 37 characters over the display limit. Google will cut it mid-sentence in search results, likely after "4 locations open 24 hours. 100+ machines." — dropping the Cash · Card · Tap line entirely. Also contains "Allison Rd" which should be "Allison St."
→ Trim to ≤155 chars and fix "Allison Rd" → "Allison St"
5 min
High
0 of 14 images have loading="lazy"
Every image on the page loads eagerly, including location exterior shots far below the fold. This directly hurts Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) — a Core Web Vitals signal Google uses in ranking. The hero images can stay eager; everything else should be lazy-loaded.
→ Add loading="lazy" to all images except the first hero image
15 min
Medium
Title is 65 chars — 5 over the soft limit
Google's title display typically truncates around 60 characters in desktop results and slightly less on mobile. "| Open 24/7, 6 Locations" gets cut first. Either trim the pipe section or rearrange to front-load the keyword.
→ Consider: "Laundromat in Cincinnati | Super Laundry — Open 24/7" (53 chars)
5 min
Medium
/partner URL slug doesn't match canonical
The Partnership Opportunities page is served at /partner in HubSpot but the HTML canonical tag points to /partnership-opportunities. Google will index /partner as the live URL but see a canonical pointing elsewhere — creating a signal conflict. Update the canonical in the HTML to match the actual HubSpot slug.
→ Change canonical in partnership-opportunities.html to https://superlaundry24.com/partner
2 min
Low
"Four Ways to Get It Done" H2 has no keyword value
The services section H2 is engaging brand copy but passes on an opportunity to reinforce the page's keyword targets. A minor rewrite can preserve the voice while adding semantic value for service-related searches.
→ Consider: "Self-Service, Drop-Off, Pickup & Delivery — Four Ways to Do Laundry in Cincinnati"
2 min
