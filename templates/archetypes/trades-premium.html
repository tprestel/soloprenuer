How We Work Together

A deliberate process,
from first conversation to final detail.

1
Discovery & Design

We begin with a thorough in-home consultation. We listen first, then translate your vision into a detailed design scope.

2

Our design team guides you through curated selections for cabinetry, tile, fixtures, and finishes — no decision is made alone.

Sarah & James Harlow — Founders

The Studio

Led by craft.
Driven by detail.

Sarah and James Harlow founded this firm on a single conviction: that a truly great renovation demands both the imagination of a designer and the precision of a builder. After nearly two decades in high-end residential work, they brought both under one roof.

Every project is personally led by one of the founders. That means a single point of vision, from concept to final reveal — no handoffs, no disconnects. The homes they build are ones their clients plan to grow old in. That weight is something they carry into every decision they make.
