Our Story

Cooking with
conviction.

James Calloway spent twelve years working beside wood-burning hearths in kitchens across Lyon, Barcelona, and Charleston before opening Ember & Oak in 2019. The restaurant began as a simple belief: that fire, handled with patience, brings out a truth in ingredients that no other method can.

The menu changes with the seasons — sometimes week to week — because the market dictates everything. A spring delivery of young garlic might reshape a sauce. A late summer haul of stone fruit might appear three ways in a single evening. Nothing is static. Nothing is predictable.

What remains constant is the care. The oak logs sourced from a single farm in the Shenandoah Valley. The bread made each morning. The wine list built around producers who farm as thoughtfully as we cook.
