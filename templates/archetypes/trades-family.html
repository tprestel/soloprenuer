Our Story
“My grandfather told me: do the job right the first time, and you'll never need to explain yourself.”

— Tom Calloway, Owner & Third-Generation Electrician

Harold Calloway started this company out of a one-van operation in Hyde Park in 1978. He worked every job himself, brought his son Robert along on summers, and built a reputation that spread neighborhood to neighborhood purely by word of mouth. Robert joined full-time in the late 1990s and helped the business grow from a solo operation to a full crew. Today his sons, Tom and Danny, run service calls and estimate jobs the same way Harold taught them — show up on time, explain what you're doing, and never cut corners. Over 45 years, three generations, and 12,000+ homes later, that hasn't changed.
