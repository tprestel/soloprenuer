Our Mission

We believe no one should go hungry. No one should go untrained. No one should be left out.

The Harbor Project started as a single food pantry in the West End. Today, we run three integrated programs that meet people where they are — with meals, job skills, and support for their children — and walk alongside them toward something better.

We don't just address symptoms. We build relationships, open doors, and stay for the whole journey.

What We Do

Three programs. One commitment.

Food Security

A weekly food pantry, hot meal service, and fresh produce distribution that keeps 4,200 families fed — no judgment, no paperwork, no waiting list.

↑ 4,200 families served monthly

Learn More →

Workforce Training

Eight-week cohort programs in hospitality, healthcare support, and skilled trades — with direct job placement and employer partnerships that stick.

↑ 320 jobs placed this year

Learn More →

Youth Enrichment

After-school tutoring, summer camps, and mentorship for kids ages 6–17 — because every child deserves a safe place to grow, learn, and imagine more.

↑ 680 youth enrolled annually
