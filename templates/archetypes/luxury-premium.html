On Discretion

Every engagement begins with listening. We spend weeks understanding a client's life before we ever suggest a property. The addresses we bring to our clients are never listed publicly — they are offered privately, to those we believe belong there.

On Curation

We work with a small number of clients at any given time. This is not a constraint — it is a commitment. When we take on a search, we give it everything. No brief goes unseen, no property passes without scrutiny. We have never recommended a home we wouldn't live in ourselves.

On Legacy

Our clients do not purchase square footage. They acquire something rarer: a place that will hold their family's story. Our work is complete only when we are certain that the home and the people inside it are truly matched — not in preference, but in kind.

03   How We Work
I

Discovery

We begin not with properties, but with people. An extended conversation — unhurried and entirely confidential — to understand how you live, what you value, and what a home must give you before it can be considered right.

II

Curation

From our private network of off-market properties, we select no more than five addresses for your consideration. Each is presented with full provenance, a detailed brief, and our honest assessment of its suitability for you.

III

Placement

Once a match is made, we manage every detail of the transition — negotiation, legals, surveys, and the quiet arrangements that transform a house into a home. We remain available long after the keys are turned.

04   The Team

Marcus Webb,
Founder

Marcus spent fifteen years as an architectural historian before he ever brokered a sale. He came to property not through commerce but through a deep belief that the spaces we inhabit shape the lives we lead — and that the reverse is also true: the lives we intend to live must determine the spaces we choose.

Meridian was founded on a single principle: that luxury in real estate is not measured in price per square foot, but in the quality of the match between a family and its home. Marcus works with no more than twelve clients per year. Each engagement is personal, private, and unhurried.

He is joined by a small team of advisors — specialists in architecture, interior provenance, and estate law — who share his conviction that the finest homes are not sold but entrusted.
