Recommended when the existing platform is crawl-limited (e.g., Wix, Squarespace), the site is structurally outdated, or the volume of issues makes a rebuild more efficient than patching.

**Page count is determined by running `website-builder` Gate 1 (site architecture) before the proposal. Always run Gate 1 first — do not quote a rebuild price until the architecture is complete.**

**HubSpot Setup (included in all tiers):**
- HubSpot CRM configured — every form submission automatically captured as a contact
- Lead pipeline set up (New Inquiry → Contacted → Proposal Sent → Closed)
- Contact forms on the site connected to HubSpot CRM
- Source tracking — know whether leads came from Google, GBP, a blog post, or direct
- Meeting scheduler integration (replaces back-and-forth email for booking calls)
- Basic email template for inquiry follow-up

**The pitch:** Every business running on HubSpot knows exactly where their leads come from, has every contact in one place, and never loses a follow-up. Most small businesses in this space track leads in their head or a spreadsheet. This is the infrastructure that changes that.

## Decision Guide: Rebuild vs. Optimize

Recommend a **Website Rebuild** when 2+ of the following are true:
- Platform is crawl-limited (Wix, Squarespace, Weebly, heavy JavaScript)
- Website score is 1–3/10
- Number of issues is so large fixing them in-place approaches rebuild cost
- Site design is 5+ years outdated
- Service page content is thin or placeholder-level

Recommend **Optimize in Place** when:
- Platform is adequate (WordPress, well-configured CMS)
- Issues are structural (missing H1s, meta, schema) but content is solid
- Client wants to move fast without a full site project
