## Short Description (132 characters max — this shows in search results)

## Full Description
CleanNote gives you a clean writing space with one click.

No sign-ups. No accounts. No distractions. Just open it and write.

HOW IT WORKS
• Click the CleanNote icon in your toolbar to open your notepad
• Start typing — your notes save automatically as you write
• Close the tab and come back anytime — your notes are always there

FEATURES
• Autosave — notes save every time you pause typing
• Three themes — light, dark, and sepia
• Customizable — choose your font, size, and line height
• Word count — always visible in the status bar
• Import — load .txt or .md files into your notepad
• Export — download your notes as .txt or .md anytime
• Keyboard shortcuts — Ctrl+S to save, Tab to indent
• Single tab — clicking the icon focuses your existing note instead of opening duplicates

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
CleanNote stores everything locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. No analytics. No tracking. No data collection of any kind.

Built for writers, note-takers, students, and anyone who wants a calm place to think.
