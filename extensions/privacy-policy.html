Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the TabQuill and TabSafe Chrome extensions.

Summary

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your data. Period.

Data Storage

Both extensions store data exclusively on your device using Chrome's built-in local storage API (chrome.storage.local). This includes:

  • TabQuill: Your note content, display settings (font, theme, size)
  • TabSafe: Your saved tab groups, backup copies, display settings

This data never leaves your browser. It is not sent to any server, API, or third party.

Data Collection

We collect no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information
  • No browsing history
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No cookies (beyond Chrome's built-in storage)
  • No network requests of any kind
  • No tracking pixels or fingerprinting

Third-Party Services

These extensions do not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, or external APIs.

Permissions

The extensions request only the minimum permissions needed to function:

  • storage — to save your notes and tab groups locally on your device
  • tabs (TabSafe only) — to read your open tabs when you choose to save them

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above. Given our commitment to local-only storage, we do not anticipate changes.
